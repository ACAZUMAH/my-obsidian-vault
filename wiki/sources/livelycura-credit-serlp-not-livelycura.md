# LivelyCura Credit SERLP Not LivelyCura

## Source

- Raw file: [Product credits say SERLP, not LivelyCura](../../raw/sources/2026-11-24-livelycura-credit-serlp-not-livelycura.md)
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Author: Product owner (verbatim)
- Ingested: 2026-11-24

## Core Claims

- Credit lines in the SERLP products name SERLP, never LivelyCura:
  - SERLP Team sign-in footer: "SERLP Team · by SERLP"
  - serlp-mobile More version line: "SERLP Mobile v… · by SERLP"
  - portal PDF footer: "Generated by SERLP"
  - receipts already said "Powered by SERLP"
- LivelyCura stays only where it names a real LivelyCura service, such as "LivelyCura delivery" on marketplace orders.

## Useful Details

- The owner spotted "SERLP Team · by LivelyCura" on the sign-in screen right after the Loop rename and called it wrong. They then approved the same change on serlp-mobile's More screen.
- The portal PDF footer ("Generated by LivelyCura ERP") was changed under the same rule.

# SERLP AI Tab And Chats Drawer Direction

## Source

- Raw file: [Direction: SERLP AI stays a tab that opens its own full screen; chats drawer pushes the chat aside](../../raw/sources/2026-11-20-serlp-ai-tab-and-chats-drawer-direction.md)
- Raw file: [Direction: SERLP AI chats drawer copies the Claude and ChatGPT apps; icons go back](../../raw/sources/2026-11-20-serlp-ai-drawer-reference-and-icons-reversal.md)
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Author: The owner (paraphrased), relayed by the Claude Code session
- Ingested: 2026-11-20

## Core Claims

- In serlp-mobile, SERLP AI stays a bottom tab; tapping it slides a full-screen AI screen (no tab bar) in from the right, and its back button returns to the tabs. There is no AI button in the Apps header and no swipe between the tabs and the AI screen.
- The chats drawer copies the Claude and ChatGPT iPhone apps: a swipe right or the ☰ button slides the chat right as a card with rounded corners, a thin edge and a dim, while the chats list underneath stays put and fades in (about 80% wide).
- The drawer has "SERLP AI" with a round search button at the top, bold group titles, plain one-line chat titles, and at the foot the organization (logo and name) with this period's credits beside a New chat pill.
- Send is the arrow-up and New chat the boxed pencil, as in those apps. (The owner first asked for a paper plane and a plain pencil the same day, then reversed it.)
- In the owner's action descriptions, "tab" means "tap"; when a phrase could mean two different builds, take the closest literal reading before building.

## Useful Details

- Trigger: the owner wrote "when I tab slide the screen from right to left"; the agent built a swipe-opened page, removed the tab and added a header button, which the owner rejected.
- The AI screen's overall look follows the Claude and ChatGPT mobile apps (greeting with starter questions, one rounded composer, plain replies).
- The owner sent screenshots of both drawers and a recording of ChatGPT's after a first push-style drawer (both sides sliding, search field and pencil at the top, dated rows with bins) was "still not there yet". Studying the recording frame by frame showed the list stays underneath and fades in.

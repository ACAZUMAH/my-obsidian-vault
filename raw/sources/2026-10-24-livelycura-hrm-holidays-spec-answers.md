# LivelyCura HRM: holidays build-spec answers

- Date: 2026-10-24
- Author: the product owner, answering the two open questions of the Holidays build spec in a LivelyCura Claude Code session
- Recorded from: the owner's own answer messages (quoted verbatim)
- Status when recorded: decided, not yet built

## Answers (verbatim)

1. Open-shift pickup on a holiday: "only the picked-up shift counts; their usual shifts that day stay excused unless a manager marks them as working."
2. Clock-in by someone not marked as working on a holiday: "allow the clock-in and record it, but flag it on the Who's working page as 'Clocked in but not marked' with a one-click 'Mark as working'; holiday pay will later follow the working list."

## Context given to the owner before answer 2

A holiday means staff are off by default, not that the pharmacy closes; pharmacies often open on holidays. Unmarked clock-ins come from forgotten marking, holidays entered late after short-notice announcements, and informal swaps. Refusing would lock such people out until a manager marks them; allowing without a flag would let unasked work count. Approved leave already refuses clock-in.

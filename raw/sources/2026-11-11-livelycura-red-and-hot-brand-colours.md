# LivelyCura Red and Hot app: brand colours and icon

Date: 2026-11-11
Source: the owner's instructions in a Claude Code session, with the supplied logo (paraphrased).

The owner supplied the real logo: the word "RED&HOT" in red italic brush lettering on white. Instructions: the brand colours are red and white for light mode and red and black for dark mode; use the R&H letters to make the app icon; change the app's look to red and white; replace the logo on the Menu tab with an icon; round the top left and right corners of the bottom tab bar.

Built the same day: logo red #F20304 (from the file), #DC0304 for buttons and small text on red in light mode (white on #F20304 is only 4.38:1), #FF4040 as the dark-mode red; white or black backgrounds with no cream, brown, orange or paper grain; red price stickers and brush strokes; app icon of white R, & and H cut from the logo on the logo red; adaptive, notification and splash assets from the same lettering; a fork-and-knife Menu tab icon; 22 pt rounded top corners on the tab bar. Checked in the simulator in light and dark; the icon and splash need a new native build to appear.

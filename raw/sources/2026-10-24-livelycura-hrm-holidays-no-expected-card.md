# LivelyCura HRM: holidays, no "Still to be announced" card

- Date: 2026-10-24
- Author: the product owner, after seeing the built card on the portal Holidays page in a LivelyCura Claude Code session
- Recorded from: the owner's own message (quoted verbatim)
- Replaces: the card part of decision 2 in 2026-10-23-livelycura-hrm-holidays-decisions.md

## The owner's words (verbatim)

"the one you put on the card beneath the header, how about we remove them I mean if it get to that time of the year and the announce it the hr will get create them and assign employees who will be off, better than showing it there. if they didn't add it it means it wasn't a holiday for that org"

## What it changes

- No "Still to be announced" card for Eid-ul-Fitr, Shaqq Day or Eid-ul-Adha on any screen, and none of its supporting estimates or hiding.
- HR creates Eid and other announced days with New Holiday when they are declared. A day HR doesn't add was not a holiday for that organization.
- Decision 1 is unchanged: once a holiday exists everyone is off, and HR only marks who is working (the session pointed this out in reply to "assign employees who will be off").

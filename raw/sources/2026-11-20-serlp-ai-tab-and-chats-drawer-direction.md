# Direction: SERLP AI stays a tab that opens its own full screen; chats drawer pushes the chat aside

Date: 2026-11-20
Source: the owner's messages in a Claude Code session on the serlp-mobile redesign (paraphrased; expletives left out), plus the session facts that led to them.

## What happened

- The owner asked to "remove" SERLP AI "from a bottom tab screen to its own screen, so when I tab slide the screen from right to left", and for the AI screen to look like the Claude or ChatGPT mobile apps.
- The agent read "tab slide" as a swipe: it took SERLP AI out of the tab bar, made it a page opened by swiping right-to-left, and added an AI button to the Apps header beside the bell.
- The owner replied that they had asked for its own screen, not for the tab to be removed and turned into a button: bring the tab back and remove the header button.
- The owner also said the chats drawer did not work as wanted: in the Claude and ChatGPT apps, swiping or tapping the drawer button slides the chat screen to the right while the drawer slides in at the same time. They asked for a different send icon (not the arrow-up) and a plain pencil for New chat (not the boxed pencil).

## Direction

- SERLP AI stays in the bottom tab bar. Tapping its tab slides a full-screen SERLP AI screen (no tab bar) in from the right; its back button returns to the tabs. No AI button in the Apps header and no swipe between the tabs and the AI screen.
- The chats drawer is push style, as in the Claude and ChatGPT apps: a swipe to the right or the ☰ button moves the chat to the right while the drawer slides in beside it, together; a swipe back or a tap on the chat closes it.
- Send uses a paper-plane style icon, not an arrow-up; New chat is a plain pencil.
- When the owner describes an action, "tab" means "tap". When a phrase could mean two different builds, take the closest literal reading of the owner's words before building.

# LivelyCura independent Fleet decision

Source: direct user instructions in the Codex Fleet rebuild task, 2026-09-17. This excerpt preserves the product decision, not temporary implementation progress.

> so right now the courier we have on the pharmacy portal is not right so we going scrap that off of the pharmacy portal and the serlp mobile. And that particular courier service feature now is going to be managed by livelycura in the admin portal, for standalone riders, delivery tasks that comes from the marketplaces and custom apps and requesting rides which the riders use the serlp dispatch to fulfill them.

> now what coming to do it to build a fleet management system feature which is going to replace what we have entirely on the pharmacy portal, which has nothing to do with what we have already. It's going to be a standard fleet system for fleet or courier companies to manage the vehicles, riders, tasks, rides and any other operations you can thing of for fleet or courier company.

> there can be a case where maybe the fleet company want to opt in and take delivery request from livelycura to fulfill them that's fine to but the we going to separate them.

> so let's do our research and plan on how to rebuild it. first we will have to do the clean up first and after that then start adding the new feature

The subsequent user instruction was: “implement the plan”.

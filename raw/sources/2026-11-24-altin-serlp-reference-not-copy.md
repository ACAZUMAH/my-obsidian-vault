## Reading

- The SERLP Mobile redesign (serlp-mobile `dbd1a835`, board `livelycura-hq/tasks/mobile-ui-redesign.md`, handoff `livelycura-hq/docs/mobile-ui-redesign-2026-11/handoff.md`) is the quality bar and the method the user approved. It is not the target look for Altin.
- Altin Partner Mobile does a different job (delivery and dispatch partner operations), so its screens, data and identity stay its own. SERLP shows how to improve; Altin decides what to improve.
- The method in that board:
  - Tab by tab, with the owner checking each.
  - Glass only on floating controls; solid content; header fades; list ends clear of the tab bar.
  - Light mode on neutral greys: no harsh outlines, a soft shadow, inset hairlines, near-black text, darker status words. Dark mode untouched.
  - A black modal dim that fades in.
  - An audit of every module for crowded or repetitive screens, each reworked keeping every fact.
  - An independent review per area, and checks on several devices.

# Fleet Operator Detail Information Scope

- Date: 2026-09-21
- Author: User
- Context: LivelyCura Pharmacy Portal Fleet operator detail page

## Direct Correction

- Follow the information choices used by established project detail pages.
- Do not present database IDs, user-account IDs, organization IDs, facility IDs, duplicate operator IDs, routine created/updated timestamps, or inactive archive placeholders as operator details.
- The detail page should show information that helps a fleet manager understand and operate the person record.

## Durable Interpretation

The routed Fleet operator detail page presents the operator's business profile, engagement, compliance documents, live work state, capacity, activity, and location. Internal persistence and tenancy identifiers stay out of the visible record. Archive context is relevant only when the operator is archived.

# LivelyCura: product credits say SERLP, not LivelyCura (decided 2026-11-24)

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Author: the product owner, in a LivelyCura Claude Code session
- Recorded from: the owner's own messages (quoted verbatim below)
- Feature: credit lines in the SERLP products (sign-in footer, version line, document footers)
- Status when recorded: decided and built locally (not pushed)

## Context

After the staff app Loop was renamed SERLP Team, its sign-in screen footer read "SERLP Team · by LivelyCura". serlp-mobile's More screen read "SERLP Mobile v1.1.5 · by LivelyCura", and portal PDFs ended with "Generated by LivelyCura ERP". Portal receipts already said "Powered by SERLP".

## The owner's words (verbatim)

- "I am seeing serlp team by livelycura on the log in screen that's wrong so change it, it should by serlp"
- On being asked whether serlp-mobile's More screen should also say "by SERLP" instead of "by LivelyCura": "Yes, change it"

## What was changed

- SERLP Team sign-in footer: "SERLP Team · by SERLP".
- serlp-mobile More screen version line: "SERLP Mobile v… · by SERLP".
- Pharmacy portal PDF footer: "Generated by SERLP". The session applied the same rule here as a leftover of the same kind.
- "LivelyCura delivery" (LivelyCura's real marketplace delivery service) is a service name, not a product credit, and stays.

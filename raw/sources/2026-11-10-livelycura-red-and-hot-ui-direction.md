# LivelyCura Red and Hot app: UI direction chosen

Date: 2026-11-10
Source: the owner's decision in a Claude Code session in `/Users/acazumah/Documents/LivelyCura`, after reviewing three HTML phone-mockup directions (paraphrased; the quoted phrase is the owner's own words).

## Directions shown

Three directions were built as clickable phone mockups in `/Users/acazumah/Documents/LivelyCura/design-probes/red-and-hot-app` (outside the app repository), each showing Menu, Item detail, Cart with Checkout, and Orders in light and dark:

- A "Ember": charcoal grill house, ember red and coal orange, condensed capital headings.
- B "Chop Bar": warmth of a hand-painted Ghanaian food signboard, paper-cream background, chilli red, palm-oil orange price stickers, Lilita One headings and Nunito body text, roasted-brown dark mode.
- C "Clean Heat": white surfaces, one hot red only on tappable things, one geometric font.

Two review agents ranked A first; the session recommended A.

## Owner's decision

The owner chose B, because the UI should look "totally different from the check city app". In B, the price sticker and the add button on product cards are fine as they are. Three parts still look like Check City and will be redesigned:

- the bottom tab bar (Check City's floating rounded track with a growing filled pill and a separate round Cart button);
- the product card image (Check City's round photo overlapping the top of the card);
- the product view screen (item detail).

The tab destinations (Menu, Search, Orders, Cart) and every screen's content stay the same; only the look changes.
